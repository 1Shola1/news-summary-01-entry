At COP31 in Antalya, UN Secretary-General António Guterres appealed for stronger climate action. The Associated Press and Reuters reported that leaders of the United States, China and India were absent. In Abu Dhabi, Ukraine and Russia agreed to a large prisoner-of-war exchange, but talks made no major breakthrough toward ending the war; details of the exchange were expected Thursday, the Associated Press reported.

Reuters reported that NATO scrambled jets after at least four unidentified drones entered Baltic airspace during Ukrainian strikes on Russia. Lithuania, Latvia and Estonia issued shelter alerts; no injuries were reported. The BBC reported that US prosecutors charged three men over alleged Iran-backed plots in the United States and Europe, including an alleged plot involving RAF Fairford in Britain. The charges are allegations, not convictions; Iran denied involvement.

In DR Congo, government data cited by Reuters showed 11,042 confirmed Bundibugyo-strain Ebola cases and 5,387 deaths across eight affected provinces. Reuters reported that local health officials said Israeli strikes in Gaza killed at least seven people, including a father and his 13-year-old son; the Israeli military said it targeted militants.
